Our mission

Avialae Labs builds technology that steps beyond the screen and into the physical world, so everyone, at any age and with any comfort with tech, can live with machines that understand them, act for them, and keep what's theirs private.

Meet people where they are

Homes are full of speakers, thermostats, TVs and locks, each with its own app and login. The features are there; using them is the hard part. We build for the grandparent with five remotes, not just the early adopter.

Act, don't just answer

Today's assistants set timers and answer trivia, and running a household is still manual. Our technology does the thing: the device, the booking, the reminder, the follow-through.

Keep what's yours

A capable assistant shouldn't need your whole life in someone else's cloud. The thinking stays on your own devices by default, and nothing personal leaves them without asking.

Avialae is the scientific name for the group that includes every flying bird. We're a multidisciplinary lab, and EVIE is our first product. We start in the living room, and over time we'll aim higher.

Meet EVIE.

Say what you want the way you'd say it to a person. EVIE works out the rest, with a dry sense of humor and none of the menus.

I'm cold.

Turns up the thermostat.

Turn on the news.

Switches on a 15-year-old TV through infrared, no smart TV needed.

Movie night.

Dims the lights and starts the TV.

Book dinner Friday for four.

Makes the restaurant reservation.

Anything from school?

Reads the school emails so nobody misses the field-trip form.

Where's my package?

Tracks the delivery and says when it's at the door.

Works with the home you have

Wi-Fi lights and plugs, Google Nest, Philips Hue, SmartThings, Home Assistant and more, plus older TVs, fans and air conditioners through EVIE's infrared hub. Your home doesn't have to be "smart."

Private by default

EVIE's AI runs on your own computer. Only genuinely hard questions go to the cloud, one request at a time, and EVIE asks before anything personal leaves. Monitoring is off unless you turn it on.

Nothing to set up

One installer. Connecting an account means signing in, with no technical setup and nobody to call. Many devices are found automatically.

Careful with what matters

Locks, garage doors, alarms and cameras always need a spoken "yes," and EVIE never touches them on its own. Up to four people can share a household, each with private data.
