Our mission

Avialae Labs builds technology that steps beyond the screen and into the physical world, so everyone, at any age and with any comfort with tech, can live with machines that understand them, act for them, and keep what's theirs private.
Meet people where they are
Homes are full of speakers, thermostats, TVs and locks, each with its own app and login. The features are there; using them is the hard part. We build for the grandparent with five remotes, not just the early adopter.
Act, don't just answer
Today's assistants set timers and answer trivia, and running a household is still manual. Our technology does the thing: the device, the booking, the reminder, the follow-through.
Keep what's yours
A capable assistant shouldn't need your whole life in someone else's cloud. The thinking stays on your own devices by default, and nothing personal leaves them without asking.

Avialae is the scientific name for the group that includes every flying bird. We're a multidisciplinary lab, and EVIE is our first product. We start in the living room, and over time we'll aim higher.

Works with the home you have

Your home doesn't have to be "smart." EVIE talks to Wi-Fi lights and plugs and the big platforms, and its infrared hub learns the remotes for older TVs, fans and air conditioners.

Private by default

EVIE's AI runs on your own computer. Only genuinely hard questions go to the cloud, one at a time, and it asks before anything personal leaves.

Nothing to set up

One installer. Connecting an account means signing in, and many devices are found on their own.

Careful with what matters

Locks, garage doors, alarms and cameras always need a spoken "yes," and EVIE never touches them on its own.
